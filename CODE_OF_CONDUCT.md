# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
ChaosTree is an open, welcoming project. We welcome participation from people of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels, and we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment‑free experience for everyone.

## Standards of Behavior
Please be friendly and patient, use inclusive language, and aim to give polite, actionable feedback. Treat others with respect and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Examples include harassment, hateful or demeaning language, threats, sharing private information without consent, sustained trolling or disruption, sexual content in project spaces, and discriminatory language or conduct.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces including GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions), mailing lists, chat channels, and events related to ChaosTree.

## Reporting an Incident
I am the sole maintainer of ChaosTree. If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it directly to me:

- Email: <YOUR_EMAIL>
- Or open a GitHub issue titled `Code of Conduct report` and mention `@Chaos-vy`. (Note: GitHub issues are public; email is preferred for confidential reports.)

When reporting, include:
- A concise description of the incident, date/time, and venue (issue, PR, chat).
- GitHub handles or names of people involved, and links to relevant content when possible.
- Any steps you would like me to take.

I will treat reports seriously and keep the reporter’s identity confidential to the extent feasible.

## Enforcement
As the project maintainer, I will review reports and take action as appropriate. Possible responses include:
- Private warning or request to change behavior.
- Temporary or permanent removal from project channels.
- Removal of commit/PR access.
- Public or private disclosure of the resolution when appropriate.

For serious or criminal behavior, I may escalate to platform support (e.g., GitHub Trust & Safety) or local authorities.

## Appeals
If you disagree with the outcome of a decision, you may request a reconsideration by contacting me at <YOUR_EMAIL>. I will seek an impartial review where feasible.
